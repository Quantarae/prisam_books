Overview & Core Principles

Welcome to PriSam Books: Magic Stories (“the App”), an interactive storytelling application designed for toddlers, early readers, and families. This Privacy Policy describes our privacy practices and explains why our app is safe, private, and fully compliant with global child protection regulations and the Google Play Families Policy.

Summary for Parents: PriSam Books does not collect, record, track, transmit, or share any personal information or device data whatsoever. The app functions completely offline without advertising or internet permissions.

Compliance with Children's Privacy (COPPA, GDPR-K & Google Play Families)

Because PriSam Books: Magic Stories is tailored for young children (including children under 13), protecting children’s safety and privacy is our highest priority:

  • COPPA Compliance: We strictly adhere to the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act. We do not knowingly collect, request, or store any personal information from children under 13.
  • GDPR-K Compliance: In compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation regarding children, no personal data or digital identifiers are processed or stored.
  • Google Play Families Policy: The app adheres strictly to Google Play’s Developer Program Policy for Families. There is no behavioral tracking, no third-party SDKs harvesting telemetry, and no inappropriate content.

Zero Data Collection (Google Play Data Safety)

Under Google Play’s Data Safety standard, we declare that no user data is collected or shared. Specifically:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, or user photos.
  • No Account or Registration: The app requires no sign-up, login, social media connection, or profile creation.
  • No Device Identifiers: We do not collect, read, or track Advertising IDs (AAID), Android IDs, MAC addresses, or IMEI numbers.
  • No Precise or Coarse Location: The app does not access, request, or track GPS or network location.
  • No Telemetry or Usage Analytics: There are no analytics SDKs (such as Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, or third-party trackers) embedded in the app.
  • No Financial or Payment Information: The app contains no in-app purchases or payment processing mechanisms.

Offline Operation & Device Permissions

PriSam Books: Magic Stories is designed as a standalone, offline application:

  • No Internet Permission: The production app does not declare or request the Android android.permission.INTERNET permission. It does not establish outbound connections or contact remote servers.
  • Local Media Playback: All story pages, animations, illustrations, and narrated audio files are bundled locally inside the application package.
  • On-Device Text-to-Speech (TTS): Narration and read-aloud functionality rely entirely on local audio files and the device’s local built-in speech engine without transmitting text over the network.
  • No Sensor or Camera Access: The app does not access the device camera, microphone, contact list, or photo library.

Advertising & In-App Purchases

To provide a tranquil and safe reading environment for young readers:

  • 100% Ad-Free: The app does not display any advertisements, pop-ups, banners, or interstitial promotions.
  • No Ad SDKs: The app contains no third-party advertising SDKs or monetization trackers.
  • No In-App Purchases: All available stories and features are fully accessible without hidden paywalls, microtransactions, or subscription traps.

Local Device Storage & Data Deletion

The app may save small, non-personal operational preferences locally on your device (such as current reading progress, volume levels, or mute preferences):

  • This information is stored strictly within the app’s isolated local sandbox storage on your device and is never transmitted off your device.
  • Because this application does not collect, receive, or store any data on external servers, there is no remote user data to retain or delete.
  • Parents or users can reset or remove all local preferences at any time by clearing the application storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the application.

Third-Party Services

Because PriSam Books operates entirely offline, no third-party software development kits (SDKs) are employed for analytics, advertising, or profiling. App distribution and platform integrity updates are handled exclusively via the official Google Play Store platform in accordance with Google Play’s standard platform terms.

Changes to This Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect future enhancements or modifications in legal requirements. Any updates will be posted directly to this official policy page with an updated “Last Updated” date.
